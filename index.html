Tax engine

2% in, 2% out. Half feeds the pool, half funds buybacks

Once trading is live on PancakeSwap, every buy and every sell carries a flat 2% tax written into the contract. Each tax is split down the middle. Half strengthens the liquidity pool, and half goes to the creator, whose share is used for token buybacks.

Buy tax

2%

Charged on PancakeSwap purchases, taken in MBULL at the moment of the trade.

1%of the trade to the liquidity pool
1%of the trade to the creator, used for token buybacks

Sell tax

2%

Charged on PancakeSwap sales, taken in MBULL at the moment of the trade.

1%of the trade to the liquidity pool
1%of the trade to the creator, used for token buybacks
Worked example. You buy 100,000 MBULL on PancakeSwap. The 2% buy tax is 2,000 MBULL. Of that, 1,000 MBULL (50%) goes to the liquidity pool and 1,000 MBULL (50%) goes to the creator, whose share is used for token buybacks. You receive 98,000 MBULL. Minting is not a buy, so free mint claims carry no buy or sell tax, only network gas.

FAQ

Straight answers

Is the mint really free?

Yes. The token price is 0 BNB and the contract never sells MBULL. You pay only the normal BNB Chain network gas, so the 1,000 MBULL themselves cost nothing.

What is the creator share of the tax used for?

Token buybacks. Every buy and sell tax is split evenly, half to the liquidity pool and half to the creator, and the creator share is used for token buybacks.

How much can one wallet mint?

1,000 MBULL per mint, up to 10 mints per wallet, for a maximum of 10,000 MBULL. The cap is enforced by the contract per wallet address.

When does PancakeSwap trading start?

After the mint ends. At that point the PancakeSwap liquidity pool is added using the 4,000,000 MBULL liquidity reserve, and trading opens with the 2% buy tax and 2% sell tax live.

How does the tax work?

Buys and sells on PancakeSwap are taxed 2%. Every tax is split evenly, half to the liquidity pool and half to the creator, and the creator share is used for token buybacks. Ordinary transfers between wallets and mint claims are not buys or sells and carry no trade tax.

Can the supply ever increase?

No. The total supply is fixed at 20,000,000 MBULL, with 16,000,000 for the free mint and 4,000,000 for liquidity. When the mint phase ends, minting ends for good.

What is the contract address?

The official contract address is 0x544aC5e9F1e853800116eFFf1CB9f8fb0B6E8f10. It is shown at the top of this page, and you can verify it on BscScan. Treat any other address claiming to be MegaBull as unofficial, and never mint from an address you found in a comment or a DM.
